Privacy

A simple site with a simple inquiry path.

The public website is designed as a static studio site. The project form prepares an email in your own email client instead of silently submitting your brief to an Aethel database.

Project inquiry form

The form on the Contact page formats the information you enter into a mailto link and opens your preferred email application. Your message is sent only when you choose to send it through your email provider.

Email communication

If you email Aethel, the information you choose to provide may be retained in the studio email account for project communication, quoting, delivery, support, bookkeeping, or dispute resolution as reasonably needed.

Hosting and technical logs

When deployed on a hosting provider such as Vercel, that provider may process standard technical information such as IP addresses, request paths, browser details, security events, and performance logs according to its own policies.

Fonts and external services

The site may request web fonts or other public assets from external providers. Those providers can receive normal network-request information when your browser loads those resources.

Payments

If payment links or payment providers are introduced, payment details are handled by the selected provider rather than being stored directly in this static website. Provider-specific terms and privacy policies will apply.

Client artwork and confidential material

Project references, drafts, and unreleased assets are used for production and communication. Aethel will not intentionally present confidential client work as public portfolio material without permission.

Questions or deletion requests

For questions about information you have shared directly with Aethel, contact vivekvala562@gmail.com. Reasonable requests to correct or delete retained project communication can be reviewed subject to legal, payment, dispute, or recordkeeping needs.

Last updated: 8 September 2026. This notice describes the intended operating model of the site and does not replace the policies of third-party hosting, email, payment, or analytics providers.
