Studio terms

Clear expectations before production.

These are plain-language operating terms for Aethel projects. A project quote or written agreement can add or override terms for a specific commission.

Scope & deliverables

The quote defines the visual direction, number of artworks, included revision rounds, output sizes, file formats, licensing, deadline expectations, and any special crops or exports. Requests outside that scope may require a revised quote.

Revisions

Included revision rounds cover reasonable adjustments within the agreed direction. Replacing the subject, changing the core concept, switching to a substantially different style, or restarting after approval may count as a scope change.

Payment & final delivery

Payment timing is agreed before work begins. Watermarked or reduced previews may be used before final payment. Final unwatermarked master delivery occurs according to the agreed payment stage.

Licensing

Commercial-use licensing is project-scoped. Copyright transfer, exclusivity, merchandise rights, resale rights, or broad sublicensing are not assumed unless explicitly agreed. Rights in client-supplied trademarks, characters, logos, references, or other third-party material remain the client's responsibility.

AI-assisted production

Aethel may use AI-assisted generation, compositing, retouching, upscaling, and other digital tools. The studio does not guarantee that a third-party platform, marketplace, publisher, or jurisdiction will treat AI-assisted work in a particular legal or policy category.

Resolution & technical claims

4K and 8K are the current verified standard master tiers. Higher-resolution files may be offered only after project-specific feasibility and QA review. A large pixel dimension is not represented as proof of native detail.

Portfolio display

Aethel may ask for permission to display completed work as a portfolio example. Confidential or unreleased projects are not presented publicly without authorization.

Third-party policies

The client remains responsible for platform rules, publisher requirements, advertising policies, trademark clearances, print specifications, and any other downstream rules that apply to the final use.
