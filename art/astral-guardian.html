Fantasy / Character Key Art

Astral Guardian

A portrait-led fantasy direction built around luminous armor, celestial motifs, restrained color contrast, and a commanding but clean silhouette. The image is designed to work as premium character art rather than a busy narrative scene.

Suitable applications
  • Fantasy book-cover concepts
  • Game-character launch or splash art
  • Collector cards and lore material
  • Author promotional campaigns
  • Premium character posters and worldbuilding assets
Visual QA highlights
  • Face, eyes, and symmetry reviewed at 100% crop
  • Armor filigree and repeated ornament patterns inspected
  • Hair, silhouette, and material edges checked for halos
  • Hands/weapon-contact regions reviewed for geometry defects
Direction note

This is a self-initiated showcase character, not an adaptation of a client's existing IP and not a paid publishing case study.
