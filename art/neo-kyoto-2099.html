Cyberpunk / Game Key Art

Neo-Kyoto 2099

A rain-soaked futuristic city designed to balance dense visual worldbuilding with a readable cinematic focal path. The direction uses neon contrast, wet-surface reflections, layered atmosphere, and traditional architectural cues without relying on a named franchise reference.

Suitable applications
  • Game splash art and Steam-facing key visuals
  • Synthwave, electronic, or ambient music artwork
  • YouTube creator hero backgrounds
  • Launch posters and collectible wallpapers
  • Website hero imagery
Visual QA highlights
  • Wet-surface reflection continuity reviewed
  • High-contrast neon edges checked for halos
  • Architectural lines and depth cues reviewed for warping
  • Fog, signage, and small-detail regions inspected at 100% crop
Direction note

This is a self-initiated showcase piece used to demonstrate visual direction and technical delivery quality. It is not presented as commissioned client work.
