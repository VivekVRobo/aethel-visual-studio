Resolution standard

A bigger file only matters if the detail survives.

The QA process focuses on whether the delivered master remains visually useful at the requested size. Large dimensions alone are not treated as proof of real detail.

Resolution honesty

Aethel does not describe an enlarged 24K canvas as automatically containing meaningful 24K detail. Higher-resolution delivery is accepted only when the final file remains visually defensible for the requested use.
