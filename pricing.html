Common questions

Before you invest.

Do I need to pay the full amount before seeing anything?

The workflow can use a scoped payment stage appropriate to the project. Watermarked previews protect the production work while giving you a checkpoint before final master delivery. Exact payment timing is agreed in the quote.

Is 8K always better than 4K?

No. If the artwork is for a website or social post, a clean 4K file may be more useful. 8K is valuable when the final use, source detail, display size, or cropping needs justify it.

Can you provide 16K or 24K?

Higher-resolution files can be evaluated project by project, but Aethel does not market native-detail 16K/24K as a guaranteed standard. The source detail and QA result must justify the output.

Are commercial rights included?

Commercial-use licensing is available and scoped per project. Client-supplied IP, trademarks, characters, and third-party references remain subject to the rights the client actually holds.

Do you do professional video editing?

Not as a current core Aethel service. The studio is positioned around still visual artwork, art direction, high-resolution master preparation, and related image deliverables.
