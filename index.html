Process

A small studio workflow with clear checkpoints.

You see direction before final delivery, revisions stay scoped, and the master only moves forward after visual QA.

01 / BRIEF

Define the outcome

Purpose, audience, visual mood, aspect ratio, references, deliverables, and licensing needs.

02 / DIRECTION

Build the visual language

Composition, palette, atmosphere, subject hierarchy, and style direction are established.

03 / REFINE

Review & adjust

Watermarked previews create a controlled feedback loop before master preparation.

04 / MASTER

Inspect & deliver

Final output is reviewed at 100% crop and packaged for the agreed use case.

Why this is investable

Pay for visual direction and a finished asset—not for a prompt.

Aethel combines brief translation, selection, compositing/retouching where needed, high-resolution preparation, and manual QA. AI-assisted tools may be part of the production workflow, but the client-facing product is the directed, reviewed deliverable.

How Aethel works →
Resolution honesty

4K and 8K are the verified standard tiers. Higher-resolution requests are accepted only when the source and QA result justify them.

Licensing is explicit

Commercial use is scoped to the project and depends on the client brief, references, third-party rights, and agreed package.

No false production claims

Architecture is presented as concept artwork, product imagery as concept art, and video editing is not sold as a current core service.

Start with a brief

If the project deserves a premium visual, treat the visual like part of the product.

Share the use case, mood, references, format, and deadline. You’ll receive a scoped recommendation instead of a generic package.
