About Aethel

A small studio designed to be accountable for the final image.

Aethel Visual Studio is an independent visual-art practice operated by Vivek Vala. It combines brief translation, visual direction, AI-assisted production where useful, retouching/compositing, high-resolution preparation, and manual QA into a client-facing workflow.

Aethel is early-stage. The current portfolio is made of self-initiated showcase work, not invented client campaigns or fabricated testimonials.
The point of the studio

Make AI-assisted visual production feel less disposable.

Access to image-generation tools is no longer rare. The valuable part is deciding what should be made, translating a vague brief into a coherent direction, selecting the strongest composition, fixing visible defects, preparing useful outputs, and taking responsibility for what is delivered.

Aethel is built around that layer. The client is not buying access to a prompt. The client is buying a directed visual outcome with a defined scope, revision path, QA standard, and delivery package.

The studio currently focuses on still visual artwork for games, creators, publishing, music, brands, and high-resolution display or campaign use.

Evidence before marketing

4K/8K are presented as verified standard tiers because those are the capabilities currently demonstrated. Higher-resolution claims are made only after real QA.

Showcase ≠ client history

Selected works demonstrate visual range. They are not labeled as paid case studies unless real client work exists and permission is granted.

Premium means controlled

A premium feeling should come from clarity, consistency, presentation, and execution—not from inflated claims or decorative complexity.

Transparent tooling

AI-assisted generation, compositing, retouching, and upscaling may be used depending on the brief. The production method is not hidden behind false photography or rendering claims.
