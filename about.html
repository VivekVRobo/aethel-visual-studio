A creative studio built for commercial visual production.

Aethel is a team of art directors, digital artists, retouchers and quality engineers. We produce custom visual artwork for games, brands, publishing, creators and commercial campaigns across 8 industries.

What we do

You share the project brief, references and intended use. Our team develops a visual direction, produces the artwork, reviews it with you and prepares the final masters in 4K, 8K or 16K.

The studio currently focuses on still visual artwork rather than video or animation.

Every project is assigned a dedicated art lead who manages the creative direction from the first brief through to final delivery.

Scope first

Resolution, revisions, file formats and usage terms are agreed before production begins.

Multi-stage quality review

Final masters are inspected at 100% zoom by our QA team before delivery. We check anatomy, edges, geometry and material fidelity.

Portfolio honesty

Showcase pieces are demonstration works. Client projects are shown only with explicit permission.

Dedicated project lead

Every client works with a single point of contact from brief through delivery. No handoffs between strangers.
