Process

A premium result needs a controlled path to get there.

The goal is not to surprise you at the end. Aethel uses checkpoints so creative direction, scope, rights, resolution, and revision expectations stay visible throughout the project.

Typical briefs are reviewed within one business day. Production timing is quoted after scope review; no fixed turnaround is guaranteed before the brief is understood.
01 / INTAKE

Project brief

Purpose, audience, visual references, required formats, aspect ratios, deadline, budget, licensing needs, and where the final artwork will appear.

02 / DIRECTION

Visual system

Subject hierarchy, mood, composition, palette, atmosphere, detail level, and the most relevant showcase reference are translated into a working direction.

03 / PREVIEW

Watermarked concept

A controlled preview is used to confirm direction before master preparation. This protects both the client's budget and the production workflow.

04 / MASTER

Refine, inspect, deliver

Approved work is refined, prepared at the agreed resolution, checked at 100% crop, and packaged for the target use case.

Quality gate

What gets checked before delivery.

A high pixel count does not automatically mean a useful master. The QA stage is designed to catch visual defects that become obvious when artwork is enlarged or used commercially.

A

Anatomy & subject integrity

Faces, hands, eyes, body geometry, repeated objects, warped features, and subject consistency are reviewed where relevant.

Manual inspection
B

Geometry & edges

Architecture, mechanical forms, silhouettes, edge halos, perspective consistency, texture transitions, and visible seams are checked.

100% crop review
C

Export integrity

Dimensions, file format, aspect ratio, watermark state, master readability, and agreed delivery tiers are verified before handoff.

Delivery checklist
Rights & transparency

Scope before assumptions.

Commercial-use licensing is agreed per project. Client-supplied trademarks, characters, brands, or reference material remain subject to the client's authority to use them. Aethel does not automatically promise copyright transfer or exclusivity unless separately agreed and legally supportable.

AI-assisted workflow disclosure

Generation, compositing, retouching, and upscaling tools may be used depending on the brief.

Resolution disclosure

Verified standard tiers are 4K and 8K. Higher resolutions are quoted only after source-detail and QA feasibility review.

Revision boundaries

Included revision rounds are defined in the package or quote; direction changes outside the agreed brief may require a revised scope.
